## Writing

Whenever you develop text output (a daily summary, docs, page copy, commit messages, a long answer), run the `orwell` skill on your draft before you finish, and turn passive voice active where that is clearer. Published summaries in `data/summaries/` must match the email word for word: report passive voice there, but do not edit it unless asked.
